Hi, I'm Annie.

Born and raised in Charlotte, NC, I earned my degree in Interior Architecture and Design and spent three years as a commercial designer — before a quarter-life reset and a solo trip abroad led me to Austin, TX, where growth, self-reflection, and reinvention became my north star.

Through years of trying to mold myself into what felt conventional, I realized my passion extends beyond traditional career paths. My love for social media has been present since middle school, and content creation is where creativity, storytelling, and authenticity naturally meet for me.

I use my platforms to document my twenties, explore self-development, and encourage others to listen to their inner voice — even when it means going against the grain.

My hope is simple: to help even one person feel less alone — and remind you it's okay to be exactly where you are and create your own path.
